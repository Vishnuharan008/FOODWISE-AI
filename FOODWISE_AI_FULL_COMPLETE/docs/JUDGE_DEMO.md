# 7-minute judge demo

1. Overview: show AI Core and tomorrow's demand.
2. Demand AI: run Random Forest and show prediction + range.
3. Preparation: convert demand to portions and ingredients.
4. Inventory: show shortage and purchase queue.
5. Live Consumption: enter actual service values in the final connected version.
6. Waste Intelligence: show risk, reason and cost.
7. Prediction vs Actual: show error metrics.
8. Self-Learning: explain the feedback loop.
9. Explainable AI: show why the model produced the prediction.
10. What-If: change attendance/weather/event and rerun.
11. Recommendation Engine: show the final action plan.
12. Sustainability: only show verified calculations.

Core statement:
"FOODWISE AI does not stop at predicting demand. It turns the prediction into preparation, purchasing and waste-risk actions, measures what actually happened, learns from the error, and improves the next decision."
